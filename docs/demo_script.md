# NS3 Central Demo Script

This script provides a standardized walkthrough of the NS3 Central platform for investors, founders, and potential customers.

## Overview (0:00 - 1:00)
- **Start** at the Login screen. Emphasize the security and multi-tenant foundation.
- **Action**: Log in as an Administrator.
- **Talking Point**: "NS3 Central is built to manage massive distributed infrastructure. Right from the dashboard, you get a 30,000-foot view of your entire network ecosystem."

## The Dashboard (1:00 - 2:30)
- **Action**: Land on the Dashboard. Briefly pause to let the visual impact sink in.
- **Talking Point**: "Here we see our global operations: 14 Organizations, 38 Sites, and over 500 active devices. The system is constantly ingesting telemetry."
- **Action**: Highlight the real-time Alert Timeline and the simulated dynamic charts (CPU, Memory).
- **Talking Point**: "The platform instantly surfaces anomalies, keeping operations teams ahead of catastrophic failures."

## Network Discovery (2:30 - 4:00)
- **Action**: Navigate to the Discovery wizard.
- **Talking Point**: "Let's see how easy it is to bring a new network online. Instead of manual data entry, we deploy a Collector and run a discovery sweep."
- **Action**: Run the Discovery Wizard (Simulated). Show the progress bars (Ping -> SNMP -> Inventory).
- **Talking Point**: "In seconds, the Collector scans the subnet, identifies vendors and models via SNMP, and automatically provisions them."

## Device Deep Dive (4:00 - 5:30)
- **Action**: Navigate to the Devices inventory, filter, and select a "Core Switch" or "Firewall".
- **Talking Point**: "Once a device is discovered, NS3 Central provides a unified observability pane."
- **Action**: Click through the Overview and Interfaces tabs. Show the simulated port statistics and health indicators.
- **Talking Point**: "You can see real-time interface throughput, hardware health, and historical polling data without leaving this single pane of glass."

## Topology and Alerts (5:30 - 7:00)
- **Action**: Show the Topology map (Simulated). Point out the green heartbeat indicators.
- **Action**: Switch to the Alerts Center to show active critical warnings.
- **Talking Point**: "When something goes wrong, the Alerts Center consolidates the noise into actionable tickets, mapping the failure directly back to our automated discovery and topology."
- **Closing**: "This is the foundation of NS3 Central: turning complex, fragmented network data into actionable, real-time intelligence."
